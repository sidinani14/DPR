How each KPI is scored. Each KPI is scored as achievement ÷ target × weight, capped at its weight. Example: 30 points against the 48 target scores 30 ÷ 48 × 30 = 18.75 of 30. Punctuality, DPR and accountability are all-or-nothing where the target is zero-tolerance (one late arrival forfeits that week's punctuality weight).

To pass a week you need ≥75 of 100. To pass the plan you must pass at least 4 of the 5 review weeks, and score ≥80 in Week 5 — the final week must show the standard is sustainable, not a one-off push.

What happens at the end of 30 days

Both outcomes are stated plainly so there is no ambiguity later

If you meet the plan. You come off the improvement plan and continue as Junior Interior Designer with your standing on the team unchanged. The weekly KPI review continues for a further month at a lighter touch to confirm the change has held, and your next compensation review will reflect the improvement directly.

If you do not meet the plan. Your continuation in the role will be reconsidered, and any future compensation increase will be assessed strictly against whatever this plan actually demonstrates — not this cycle's baseline.

This is not a penalty for a bad week — it follows from what the role is. Reliability is the specific thing this plan measures: work planned in advance, delivered when promised, the studio kept informed daily, and a day's hours actually worked. Those are the basics the studio needs from anyone in this role.

You retain the right to raise any factual dispute about a weekly score at the review meeting for that week. Raise it that week — scores will not be revisited retrospectively at the end of the plan.

Support from the studio
