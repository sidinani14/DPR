You are being placed on a 30-day Performance Improvement Plan, effective from the start date below. To be clear about intent: the purpose of this plan is to give you a fair, unambiguous opportunity to correct course. We would rather you succeed at it.

How each KPI is scored. Each KPI is scored as achievement ÷ target × weight, capped at its weight. Example: 54 points against the 72 target scores 54 ÷ 72 × 25 = 18.8 of 25. Punctuality, DPR and accountability are all-or-nothing where the target is zero-tolerance (one late arrival forfeits that week's punctuality weight).

To pass a week you need ≥75 of 100. To pass the plan you must pass at least 3 of the 4 weeks, and score ≥80 in Week 4 — the final week must show the standard is sustainable, not a one-off push.

What happens at the end of 30 days

Both outcomes are stated plainly so there is no ambiguity later

If you meet the plan. You come off the improvement plan and continue as Technical Lead with your incentive and profit-share standing unchanged. The weekly KPI review continues for a further month at a lighter touch to confirm the change has held.

If you do not meet the plan. You will be removed from the incentive programme, including any share in company profits, and your continuation in the Technical Lead role will be reconsidered.

This is not a penalty for a bad week — it follows from what the role is. A share in the studio's profits is a share in its performance, and it is extended to people the studio and its clients can rely on. Reliability is the specific thing this plan measures: work planned in advance, delivered when promised, sites visited on schedule, and a day's hours actually worked. If those cannot be relied upon from its Technical Lead, the studio cannot justify a share of its profits against that role.

You retain the right to raise any factual dispute about a weekly score at the review meeting for that week. Raise it that week — scores will not be revisited retrospectively at the end of the plan.

Support from the studio

What you can expect from us during these 30 days
  • A weekly review meeting with Siddharth — your score, what moved, what did not, and what changes next week.
  • Work planned with you, not at you. If the assigned load is unrealistic, say so at the start of the week, when it can still be changed.
  • Blockers cleared quickly — raise them the day they arise and you will get a decision within 24 hours.
  • Your 10:30 start stays. It was granted to suit your circumstances and it is not being withdrawn. What is expected is that you honour it consistently and put in the full hours after it.
If something in this plan is genuinely not achievable, the time to say so is now, before it starts — not in week four.
